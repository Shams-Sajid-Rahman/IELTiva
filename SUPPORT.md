# Support Guide

For support staff answering students. It covers what the app actually does today. Support contact details aren't set yet, because the owner hasn't decided them. Escalation is in `OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md`.

**Never ask for a password or card details.**

## Scores

**Is my IELTiva score my real IELTS score?**
No. IELTiva is independent of the IELTS partners, and nothing here is an official result.

**Why don't I see a band for Listening or Reading?**
IELTiva shows your marked answers, such as 28 out of 40. It doesn't turn them into bands until there's a verified conversion table.

**Why doesn't AI feedback give me a band?**
AI Writing bands stay hidden until they've been checked against qualified human markers. Speaking AI bands aren't shown at all. The written feedback is still available.

**What does "Instructor reviewed" mean?**
A qualified instructor marked that work. It's still practice and not an official result.

## Speaking

**My recording failed.**
Check that the browser has microphone permission, and try again. If it keeps failing, note the browser and device and escalate.

**Why did my recording disappear?**
Recordings are deleted after 90 days by default, or when a student deletes them or withdraws speech consent. They can't be recovered.

## AI features

**Why am I asked for consent?**
Writing and tutor messages go to an AI provider, and recordings go to a speech provider. Nothing is sent until you agree, and you can withdraw under **Settings → Privacy**.

**The feature says it's not available.**
It may be switched off or at a daily limit. Check **Release controls** and **System health**.

## Payments

**I paid, but have no credits.**
Ask for the payment reference, shown on the status page and under **Billing**. Look it up in **Admin → Payments**, then press **Check again** to ask the gateway. If it says `needs_review`, escalate to an administrator.

**How do I get a refund?**
**Billing → Purchases → Request refund**. An administrator decides. The policy isn't final yet, so don't promise an outcome.

**Why did my credits go down?**
**Billing → Credits** lists every change. A failed AI request gives its credit back.

## Account

**Can I get a copy of my data?**
Yes: **Settings → Privacy → Request my data**. The file stays available for 7 days by default.

**How do I delete my account?**
**Settings → Privacy → Delete my account**. Deletion happens after 14 days, and can be cancelled until then. Payment records are kept with the student's identity removed. Staff accounts have to be removed by an administrator.

**I can't sign in.**
After too many wrong passwords, an account locks for a short time. During a private beta, only listed addresses can sign in.

## Passing things on

| Situation | Who |
|---|---|
| Possible data exposure, or someone else's data seen | The owner, **at once**, and record an incident |
| Charged but nothing received, or charged twice | Administrator |
| Complaint about a question or marking | Instructor |
| Something broken | Developer, with the page, time and browser |
| Legal letter or press | The owner |
